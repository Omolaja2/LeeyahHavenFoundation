About Us

Welcome to Leeyah Haven Foundation

Leeyah Haven Foundation is a female-focused health and empowerment organisation committed to creating healthier, safer, and more empowered communities for every girl and woman. We believe that every girl and woman deserves access to the knowledge, support, and opportunities needed to understand her health, make informed decisions, and build a better future.
